## Who they serve
Owners of single-family homes, townhomes, condos, and small multifamily (up to
9 units) in the service area. Two distinct caller populations the agent must
distinguish immediately: **prospective/current owners** and **prospective/current
tenants**.

## Services (owner-facing)
- Rental market analysis / pricing
- Property marketing & listing syndication
- Tenant screening (credit, criminal, rental history, employment verification —
  every occupant 18+)
- Leasing document preparation
- Move-in / move-out inspections
- Rent collection
- 24/7 maintenance & repair coordination
- Routine property inspections
- Monthly financial reporting + owner portal (24/7 access)
- Tenant eviction assistance
- No cancellation fee; no long-term lock-in after the initial setup fee

## Tenant placement
- Typical time-to-lease: 2–4 weeks depending on market, price, property type
- Screening applies to every applicant 18+ who will occupy the unit
- Fair Housing Act compliant — this must be a hard constraint on the voice agent,
  not just marketing copy (see audit doc)

## Pricing ⚠️ CONFIRM — do not let the agent quote numbers
Search results returned conflicting figures (18% vs 20% vs 8% of collected rent,
plus STR-sounding line items like "dynamic nightly rate pricing" and "noise
monitoring" that don't fit a long-term residential manager). This strongly
suggests either outdated snippets, a mixed-up short-term-rental page, or multiple
tiers that a keyword search flattened. **Recommendation: the voice agent should
never state a specific management fee, leasing fee, or setup fee out loud.**
Have it capture the lead's property details and offer to text/email the current
rate sheet or book a call with a leasing/BD specialist instead. This is standard
practice for PM voice agents anyway, since fees are usually customized by unit
count and property type.

## Portals
- Tenant portal referenced at truenestpm.com/tenants (and a managebuilding.com
  resident portal instance — common PM software, likely Buildium/AppFolio-family)
- Owner portal with 24/7 real-time access

## What NOT to put in the agent
- Do not hardcode individual staff names pulled from data-broker sites (ZoomInfo/
  RocketReach) — that data is third-party-scraped, not confirmed by the business,
  and staff turn over. Use role-based language instead: "our leasing team," "the
  maintenance coordinator," "a property manager."
- Do not hardcode the phone number or exact fees until confirmed (see ⚠️ above).
