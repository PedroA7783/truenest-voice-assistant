# Maintenance Troubleshooting: AC Not Cooling / AC Not Working

**Trigger phrases:** AC not cooling, air conditioner not working, no cold air, AC blowing warm/hot air, air conditioning broken, unit won't cool down, AC not turning on.

**When this applies:** Only for a non-emergency AC complaint. If the tenant is calling during a heat advisory or extreme heat, this is NOT a routine issue — follow the Universal Emergency Protocol instead and do not run this script. Ask or confirm this isn't a heat-advisory situation before proceeding.

## Script

Walk through these one at a time, in order. After each step, ask whether it resolved the issue before moving to the next one.

1. **Check the thermostat batteries.** Ask the tenant to replace them if the display looks dim or unresponsive.
2. **Check the air filter.** Ask them to look at the AC's air filter and replace it if it's visibly dirty or clogged — a dirty filter is one of the most common causes of weak or no cooling.
3. **Flush the condensate drain line.** Have them locate the AC's condensate drain line access point (usually a capped PVC pipe near the indoor unit) and pour a cup of white vinegar down it to clear a common clog that can shut the system down.

## If it's not resolved

If none of the steps fix it, the tenant can't attempt them, or the issue doesn't match this description at all: stop troubleshooting, tell the tenant you're getting this to the maintenance team, and hand off to Roy (transfer + internal notification, per the main prompt's Maintenance flow).

## Do not

- Do not suggest anything involving the electrical panel, refrigerant, or opening sealed equipment.
- Do not keep improvising additional steps beyond the three above — if these don't work, escalate.
