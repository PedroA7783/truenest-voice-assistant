# Maintenance Troubleshooting: Clogged Toilet

**Trigger phrases:** clogged toilet, toilet won't flush, toilet is backed up, toilet overflowing, toilet stopped up, toilet not draining.

## Step 1 — Triage first, always

Ask: **"Is it just this one toilet, or is water backing up in other drains too — other toilets, tubs, or sinks?"**

- If **more than one fixture** is affected: this is a sewage backup, not a simple clog. Stop here — do not troubleshoot. Follow the Universal Emergency Protocol instead.
- If it's **just the one toilet**, continue to Step 2.

## Step 2 — Plunge it (only if not actively overflowing)

If the bowl is at risk of overflowing right now: tell the tenant to **stop flushing** and, if they can safely reach it, **shut off the water supply valve at the base of the toilet**. Do not have them keep flushing to try to clear it. Once the immediate overflow risk is handled, this becomes a routine maintenance ticket — escalate per below.

If it's not at overflow risk: ask if they have a plunger.
- If yes: walk them through plunging with steady, firm up-and-down pressure to form a seal around the drain opening, for about 15-20 seconds at a time. Ask if it cleared after a couple of attempts.
- If no plunger, or plunging doesn't clear it: move to escalation.

## Do not

- Never suggest a chemical drain cleaner (can damage pipes and is a hazard for whoever services it next).
- Never suggest a drain snake or any tool beyond a standard plunger.

## If it's not resolved

If plunging doesn't clear it, the tenant doesn't have a plunger, or it's a multi-fixture backup: tell the tenant you're getting this to the maintenance team, and hand off to Roy (transfer + internal notification, per the main prompt's Maintenance flow).
