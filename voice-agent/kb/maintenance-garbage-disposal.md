# Maintenance Troubleshooting: Garbage Disposal Jammed / Not Working

**Trigger phrases:** garbage disposal not working, disposal jammed, disposal humming but not spinning, disposal won't turn on, disposal making a grinding noise, sink disposal stuck.

## Script

1. **Turn it off first.** Have the tenant switch the disposal off (wall switch) before doing anything else.
2. **Check the reset button.** Most disposals have a small red or black reset button on the underside of the unit, underneath the sink. Ask them to press it firmly — a soft click means it reset.
3. **Try it again.** Turn the water on, then switch the disposal back on briefly to see if it runs normally.

## Do not

- Do not have the tenant put their hand, fingers, or any tool inside the disposal — that's not something to guide over the phone. If the reset button doesn't fix it, this needs a technician, not further troubleshooting.
- Do not suggest anything involving the disposal's wiring or the switch itself.

## If it's not resolved

If the reset button doesn't fix it, the disposal is humming but not spinning, it's making a grinding/jammed noise, or there's any unusual smell: stop troubleshooting, tell the tenant you're getting this to the maintenance team, and hand off to Roy (transfer + internal notification, per the main prompt's Maintenance flow).
