# Maintenance Troubleshooting: No Hot Water

**Trigger phrases:** no hot water, water heater not working, water won't get hot, cold water only, water heater cold.

## Step 1 — Ask gas or electric first, always

Ask the tenant: **"Do you know if your water heater is gas or electric?"** (If they're not sure, ask if there's a small window with a blue flame visible near the bottom of the tank — that's gas.)

**If it's gas, or the tenant isn't sure which it is:** do not troubleshoot at all. Do not give any instructions about a pilot light. Skip straight to escalation below — this matches the standing rule against giving DIY guidance on anything gas-related.

**If it's confirmed electric:** continue to Step 2.

## Step 2 — Check the breaker (electric water heaters only)

Ask the tenant to check their breaker panel for one labeled for the water heater, and reset it if it's tripped (flip fully OFF, then back ON).

**If it trips again immediately: stop.** Don't keep resetting it — that indicates a real electrical problem, not something to keep troubleshooting over the phone.

## Do not

- Never give pilot-light, gas valve, or any gas-appliance instructions, regardless of what the tenant asks or how confident they sound about doing it themselves.
- Do not have the tenant reset the breaker more than once if it re-trips.

## If it's not resolved

If it's gas (or unknown), the breaker reset doesn't fix it, or it re-trips: stop troubleshooting, tell the tenant you're getting this to the maintenance team, and hand off to Roy (transfer + internal notification, per the main prompt's Maintenance flow).
