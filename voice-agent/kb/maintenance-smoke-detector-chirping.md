# Maintenance Troubleshooting: Smoke/CO Detector Chirping

**Trigger phrases:** smoke detector chirping, smoke alarm beeping, detector keeps beeping, CO detector chirping, alarm won't stop beeping.

## Step 1 — Confirm it's a chirp, not an active alarm

Ask the tenant to describe the sound: a **single short chirp every 30-60 seconds** is a low-battery signal — that's routine, continue below.

A **continuous loud alarm, or repeated fast beeping in sets**, means the detector may actually be sensing smoke or carbon monoxide right now. **If that's what's happening, stop — this is not a routine issue.** Ask if they see or smell smoke, or feel unwell (headache, dizziness, nausea — possible CO exposure), and follow the Universal Emergency Protocol instead of troubleshooting.

## Step 2 — Replace the battery (routine chirp only)

Ask the tenant to replace the detector's battery (commonly a 9-volt, but check the unit) and then press the test button to confirm it's working normally afterward.

## If it's not resolved

If a fresh battery doesn't stop the chirping, or the unit still won't test properly: stop troubleshooting, tell the tenant you're getting this to the maintenance team, and hand off to Roy (transfer + internal notification, per the main prompt's Maintenance flow). A detector that won't reset even with a new battery may need replacement — that's not something to keep troubleshooting over the phone.

## Do not

- Do not tell the tenant to remove or disable the detector.
- Do not continue troubleshooting if there's any chance this is an active smoke/CO detection rather than a low-battery chirp — treat that as an emergency instead.
