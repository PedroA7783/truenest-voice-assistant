# Maintenance Troubleshooting: Thermostat Unresponsive / Not Working

**Trigger phrases:** thermostat not working, thermostat blank, thermostat won't turn on, thermostat not responding, thermostat frozen, temperature won't change, thermostat screen is dark.

## Step 1 — Figure out which case this is

Ask whether the screen is **completely blank/unresponsive**, or **on but not controlling the temperature correctly**. These get different steps.

## Case A — Blank or unresponsive display

Walk through these one at a time, checking in after each:
1. **Replace the batteries.** Most thermostats take AA or AAA batteries even if the system is hardwired.
2. **Check the breaker.** Ask the tenant to check their breaker panel for a tripped breaker on the AC/heating system and reset it if so.
3. **Check the mounting.** Ask them to confirm the thermostat is seated securely on its wall base plate — it can sometimes come slightly loose.

## Case B — On, but not behaving correctly

1. **Confirm mode and setpoint.** Make sure it's set to the right mode (heat/cool/auto) and that the target temperature is actually different from the current room temperature.
2. **Wait a few minutes.** Many AC systems have a built-in delay of a few minutes before the compressor kicks in after a setting change — ask the tenant to wait and check again.

## If it's not resolved

If neither case's steps fix it, the tenant can't attempt them, or something else is going on: tell the tenant you're getting this to the maintenance team, and hand off to Roy (transfer + internal notification, per the main prompt's Maintenance flow).

## Do not

- Do not suggest anything involving the electrical panel beyond checking/resetting a breaker, or opening the thermostat or HVAC unit itself.
