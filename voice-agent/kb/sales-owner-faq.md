# Owner Sales: Frequently Asked Questions

Used by Nora on Owner Lead calls (Routing §1). Answer with the matching
entry when a caller asks one of these — don't recite the whole list
unprompted.

**Can I use my own handyman or contractor?**
Yes. TrueNest doesn't profit on maintenance or repairs, so outside vendors
the owner already trusts are welcome — just share their contact info.

**Can I hold the security deposit myself?**
Yes. TrueNest will advise on how to do that correctly under Florida real
estate law, but there's no issue with the owner holding it.

**How many inspections do you do per year?**
Four is the standard. In practice it's often more, since every maintenance
visit is also an opportunity to check on the property — at no extra charge.

**Do you handle evictions?**
Yes — the owner doesn't need to go to court, hire a lawyer, or meet the
sheriff; TrueNest handles the entire eviction process. The management fee
is paused until the tenant is paying rent again or a new tenant is placed.
The owner is responsible only for third-party charges: attorney fees, court
fees, sheriff fees, and locksmith. In Florida, an eviction typically costs
between $500 and $1,000 in those third-party costs.

**Do you collect the rent, or can I?**
TrueNest collects it, for logistics and accurate monthly reporting. Owner
disbursements go out twice a month, on either the 10th or the 25th — so if
a tenant pays late, the owner isn't stuck waiting a full monthly cycle.

**Is the late fee yours or mine?**
TrueNest collects the late fee, and it's 100% TrueNest's.

**Do I have to give you reserves for maintenance and repairs?**
No. TrueNest doesn't require the owner to hold any dollar amount in
reserve. TrueNest will always contact the owner for authorization first,
unless the property is in danger or a tenant's safety is at risk.

**Can I cancel at any time?**
Yes — 30 days' notice by email or text. It's a management agreement, not a
locked contract.

**Can I use my own real estate agent, or list the property myself?**
Yes, that's entirely the owner's choice. If using an outside agent/broker,
TrueNest just needs the new tenant's contact info and a copy of the signed
lease once the agent places a tenant.
