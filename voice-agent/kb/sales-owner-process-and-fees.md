## The pitch, in one line
TrueNest offers hands-off property management — TrueNest becomes the single
point of contact between the owner and the HOA, vendors, and tenants, so the
owner isn't juggling any of it directly.

## The process, start to finish

1. **Marketing.** If the property is vacant, TrueNest hires a professional
   photographer and produces video and marketing materials, then lists the
   property on the MLS and 75-85+ other real estate websites.
2. **Showings and applications.** TrueNest handles all showings, texts,
   emails, and incoming interest.
3. **Screening — deeper than most.** Not just a credit and criminal report:
   TrueNest contacts an applicant's previous landlords going back 5 years,
   and verifies employment directly with the employer (confirming both
   income and that the applicant isn't at risk of losing the job).
4. **Backup applicants, on purpose.** Most companies stop screening once
   they get one applicant. TrueNest keeps showing the property and
   collecting applications even after the first one comes in, so there's a
   qualified backup ready if the first applicant falls through.
5. **HOA coordination, if applicable.** If the property is in an HOA
   community, TrueNest contacts the HOA up front for their requirements and
   builds those into the screening process, to avoid surprises during
   approval.
6. **Owner makes the final call.** Once screening is complete, TrueNest
   presents the owner the full applicant profile — the owner makes the final
   decision, not TrueNest.
7. **Lease execution.** Once approved, TrueNest processes the lease, makes
   sure the tenant also gets approved by the HOA if applicable, and
   coordinates the final walkthrough and exchange of funds and keys.
   **The property management fee doesn't start until this point.**
8. **Maintenance and repairs.** TrueNest coordinates all of it. If the owner
   already has a handyman or contractor they trust, TrueNest is happy to use
   them — just share the contact info and they become the main point of
   contact for that property's repairs. TrueNest does not profit on
   maintenance or repairs: no kickbacks, no upcharging, and TrueNest will
   never present the owner with an invoice or estimate under TrueNest's own
   name — vendor invoices come from the vendor, directly, at cost. This is
   specifically to avoid any conflict of interest.
9. **Lease renewal.** Starting 60 days before a lease expires, TrueNest
   performs the year's final inspection and prepares a rental market
   analysis, then presents that to the owner and asks whether to renew.
   If yes, TrueNest handles convincing the tenant to sign another year
   instead of the owner paying another full leasing commission on a new
   tenant.

## Three tiers

TrueNest has three tiers: Basic, Full (the most common — used by about 95%
of TrueNest's clients), and Premium. They share the same overall process
above; what differs is the management fee rate/cap and, for Premium, an
added coverage plan. Management fee is only ever charged when rent is
actually collected — if rent isn't collected, no management fee that
period, on any tier.

### Full tier (most common — lead with this one)
- **$99 one-time setup fee**, when the owner becomes a TrueNest client.
- **Leasing service fee: 85% of the first month's rent** (a 15% discount off
  the standard 100%). Charged once, only when the tenant is approved, the
  lease is signed, and keys/funds have exchanged.
- **Management fee: 9.9% of collected rent**, with a **$120/month minimum**
  and a **$260/month maximum**.
- **Lease renewal fee: $249 flat.**
- No other fees. No hidden charges for handling HOA violations, contacting
  a tenant multiple times, or extra property visits — TrueNest considers
  all of that part of the job.

### Basic tier
- **Management fee: 8% of collected rent**, with the same **$120/month
  minimum** and **$260/month maximum** as the Full tier.
- Setup fee, leasing fee, and renewal fee for this tier haven't been
  confirmed for this document — if a caller on the Basic tier asks about
  those, don't guess; say a team member can confirm and offer to connect
  with Pedro, or point to https://truenestpm.com/pricing.

### Premium tier
- **Management fee: 11.9% of collected rent**, with a **$260/month
  minimum** and a **$365/month maximum**.
- Includes the **Landlord Protection Plan**, which covers three things:
  1. **Rent default:** if the tenant stops paying rent, covered up to
     **$3,000/month for up to 12 weeks**.
  2. **Eviction costs:** reimbursed up to **$5,000** in eviction expenses.
  3. **Malicious damage or theft:** covered up to **$35,000** if a tenant
     damages or steals from the property.
- Setup fee, leasing fee, and renewal fee for this tier haven't been
  confirmed for this document — same guidance as Basic: don't guess, defer
  to Pedro or https://truenestpm.com/pricing.

For anything about Basic or Premium beyond what's above, point the caller
to https://truenestpm.com/pricing or https://truenestpm.com/property-management-process.

## No lock-in

This is a management agreement, not a contract — TrueNest doesn't want an
owner to feel stuck. Cancel anytime with 30 days' written notice (email or
text), and TrueNest returns the lease, security deposit, keys, and anything
else of the owner's in TrueNest's possession.
